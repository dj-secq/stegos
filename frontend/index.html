    What the views mean

    Original shows the safe preview. Audio plays here and does not start on its own. The waveform sits under the player. A PDF stays a text card. This page does not render active PDF content.

    Channel and Bit plane show one bit of one channel. Bit 0 is the least significant bit. The left and right arrow keys move the bit. RGB stacks the three channels at that bit.

    Remap shows eight palettes derived from the file hash. Choose one to put it on the stage.

    Carved lists files pulled out by binwalk and foremost. Nothing in that list is run.

    Frames, on a GIF, lists each frame and its delay. Bit planes still use the composited preview.

    Quick runs the checks and carves when a signature is worth extracting. Deep also runs foremost and the full zsteg pass.

    jsteg, jpseek, and OpenStego say unavailable when those programs are not in the image. A missing program is not a failed file.
